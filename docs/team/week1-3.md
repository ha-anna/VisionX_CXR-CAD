## Weeks 1–3 — Topic Research and Team Formation

### Team activities
During the first three weeks, the team focused on researching possible project topics and completing the administrative steps required to begin the capstone project.

Activities included:
- Researching and discussing potential topics.
- Forming the five-person team.
- Selecting the team leader.
- Choosing a project topic and completing the topic application process.

### Outcome
These weeks established the team and project direction before detailed planning and implementation began.
